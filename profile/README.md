
# TRÓPICO

### Research Group on Tropical Climatology, Hydrology, and Meteorology

**Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM)**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **TRÓPICO**, a research group dedicated to advancing our understanding of tropical weather, climate, and hydrological processes.

Our research interests include:

- Tropical atmospheric dynamics and convection
- Tropical climate variability and climate change
- Tropical meteorology and extreme weather
- Hydroclimatology and precipitation
- Tropical cyclones and convectively coupled equatorial waves
- Hydrometeorological hazards and disaster risk

This organization hosts research projects, scientific software, educational resources, and outreach initiatives developed by members and collaborators of TRÓPICO.
