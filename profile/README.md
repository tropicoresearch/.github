## About Us

**TRÓPICO** is a research group dedicated to advancing our understanding of tropical atmospheric, climatic, and hydrological processes.

Our work focuses on the physical mechanisms governing tropical weather and climate, their variability across spatial and temporal scales, and their implications for extreme weather and hydrometeorological hazards.

We aim to foster scientific research, education, collaboration, and the communication of tropical atmospheric and climate science.

## Research Areas

Our research interests include:

- **Tropical Atmospheric Dynamics:** Atmospheric circulation, organized convection, and tropical waves.
- **Tropical Climatology:** Climate variability, climate change, and tropical climate dynamics.
- **Hydrology and Hydroclimatology:** Precipitation, atmospheric moisture transport, and hydrological variability.
- **Tropical Meteorology:** Tropical cyclones, mesoscale convective systems, and extreme weather.
- **Hydrometeorological Hazards:** Extreme precipitation, droughts, heat waves, lightning, and weather-related risks.
